# Breathe Clean

A mobile app that helps people quit vaping by tracking their progress, celebrating milestones, and offering personalized support and motivation, one step at a time.

## Overview

Breathe Clean was inspired by the growing number of high school students who vape because of social pressure, mental health struggles, or the belief that it's a safe alternative to smoking. The app gives people trying to quit a space to pace themselves and build healthier habits.

## Features

- **Daily progress tracking:** Record each day of your quit journey and watch your progress build.
- **Journal:** Write down your thoughts and feelings. Many teens vape to cope with stress or declining mental health, so putting emotions into words is a healthy outlet.
- **Bronchi, the animated lung:** A friendly mascot that cheers you on as you hit milestones, reinforcing habit-breaking.

## Built With

- React Native
- Expo
- Node.js
- TypeScript / JavaScript
- Developed in Visual Studio Code

## Roadmap

- Improved accessibility for users who struggle with the current interface
- A friendlier, less intimidating design that still respects the seriousness of vaping
- A more gamified rewards system
- Additional informative animations at milestones
